Why anyone should care

为什么值得你花十分钟

Claims about AI rewriting science are mostly vibes and survey anecdotes. This is a measurement on public data with a falsifiable design: seven flat pre-break years in an event study, a domain-level jump tested against bootstrapped CIs, a homogenization null that survived a fixed-n control where the raw series had manufactured a fake break, and an honest disclosure that the cross-field gradient is carried by Computer Science. The sharpest usable result: fingerprint density pays in citations until roughly 2.5 hits per 1,000 tokens — then it doesn't. Readability declines monotonically past 2023.

关于"AI 改写科研写作"的讨论大多停留在感觉和问卷。这是一份对公开数据的测量，设计上可证伪：事件研究里断点前七年平坦、学部级跳变有自助置信区间、同质化的"无断点"结论经过了固定样本量对照（原始序列的假断点正是被样本量制造出来的，论文里公开披露），并且诚实声明跨领域梯度主要由计算机科学承载。最锋利的可用结论：指纹密度换引用，到约每千词 2.5 次见顶；2023 后可读性单调恶化。
