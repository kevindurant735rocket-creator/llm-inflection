Four findings — and one methodological landmine

四个发现，和一颗方法学地雷

1 · The break is real, narrow and universal (level)

1 · 断点真实、狭窄而普适（水平）

Fingerprint vocabulary jumped in all four domains after 2022-11 (Physical +0.309 CI[.298,.320]; Life +0.270; Social +0.253; Arts +0.222) after seven statistically flat years. The cross-field gradient around the jump, however, is carried mainly by Computer Science — removing CS & Medicine the gradient dies (−0.017, n.s.) while every domain keeps its level break.

2022-11 后指纹词汇在全部四个学部跳变（此前七年统计平坦）。但"谁跳得更多"的跨领域梯度主要由计算机科学扛起——剔除 CS 与医学后梯度消失，各学部水平跳变依旧。这是本包刻意保守的地方：普适的是"跳了"，不是"跳的幅度排序"。

2 · Homogenization is a slow current — and the raw data lied about it

2 · 同质化是慢流 —— 而且原始数据一度"撒谎"

Pairwise within-field similarity rises 1.53%/yr with no post-2023 step (p=0.90). The landmine: raw centroid and nearest-neighbor series appear to break — because cells grew from ~370 to 900 papers and max-type statistics mechanically rise with n. At a fixed n=300 subsample both breaks vanish (p=0.40 / 0.89). We shipped the fixed-n columns, the regression and the disclosure inside the paper. If your corpus study's samples expand over time, your "LLM-era convergence" may be this artifact.

两两相似度每年上升约 1.53%（基数的），2023 后无超趋势阶跃（p=0.90）。地雷在于：原始的质心/最近邻序列看似有断点——只因单元格样本从约 370 篇扩到 900 篇，max 型统计量随 n 机械增大。固定 n=300 后断点全部消失。我们把固定 n 数据列、回归和披露原样留在论文与数据包里。样本量随年份扩张的语料研究，"LLM 时代趋同"很可能就是这个伪影。

3 · It pays — until ~2.5 per 1,000 — and it costs readability

3 · 引用有回报，约 2.5/千词见顶；代价是可读性

+0.228 log-citations per +1pp fingerprint density (CI[0.18,0.27]), with a significant negative quadratic (−0.045) turning over near 2.5 pp — a "sweet spot", not a slope. Meanwhile FK grade rises +0.28 post-2023 with the field fixed. Using more of the fingerprint is rewarded and makes abstracts harder to parse — both at once.

每 +1pp 指纹密度 +0.228 对数引用（显著负平方项，拐点约 2.5 pp/1k 词元）——是"甜点位"不是"越多越好"。同时字段固定后 FK 年级数 2023 后 +0.28。引用收益与阅读代价同时存在。

4 · The lexicon was derived, not copied

4 · 词表是推导出来的，不是抄来的

740 candidate terms come from machine-rewrite-vs-human paired statistics (dual-source, style-gated, paired-lift ≥1.5 → 65-term core); nothing is a blog list. The full table ships in data/ with per-term lift.

740 个候选词来自"机器改写 vs 人写"的配对统计（双源 ∧ 文风词 ∧ 配对提升≥1.5 → 65 词核心表），全部定义可复核，逐词提升度在 data/lexicon.json。
