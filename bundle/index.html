LLM Shift Observatory

How large language models changed what scientific abstracts sound like: empirical fingerprint-vocabulary diffusion, stylistic homogenization and citation outcomes — computed from a stratified sample of … English abstracts, 19 fields, 2015–2026. All figures are generated by the open analysis pipeline; nothing is hand-entered.

5 · Outcomes

Left: relative citations (vs. year median) by LLM-ism decile. Right: abstract readability (Flesch–Kincaid grade) by domain.

Method. Stratified random sample from Semantic Scholar Bulk API (19 fields × 12 years, frozen seed); language-screened to English. Fingerprint lexicon empirically derived via lift over a human pre-2022 baseline, using local-model and frontier-model rewrites with paired topic controls. Homogenization: frozen TF-IDF space, within-cell mean pairwise cosine. Identification: two-way FE + exposure DiD with pre-trend tests; bootstrap CIs (Efron 1979).
